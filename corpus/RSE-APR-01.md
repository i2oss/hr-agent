## 1 How to Read This Matrix

This matrix lists who approves each kind of request. "Manager" means your direct manager on the HR record. "Director" means your manager's director. "HR" means the HR Service Desk. "VP Finance" means the VP Finance, Tom Whitaker. Where a row says "none", no approval is needed, but you may still need to tell someone.

Approvals are given in the HR Portal unless a policy says otherwise. If your approver is away, their own manager can approve in their place. An approver cannot approve their own request; it goes to the next level up. The policies listed in each row hold the full rule, and this matrix only summarises who decides.

## 2 Time Off

| Request | Approver |
|---|---|
| PTO up to 10 consecutive days | Manager |
| PTO over 10 consecutive days | Director |
| Field-role PTO in the December blackout | Director |
| Floating holiday | Manager |
| Sick time | None (notify your manager) |
| Parental, bereavement, jury duty | HR (manager notified) |
| Unpaid personal leave | HR |

The rules behind these rows are in the Paid Time Off Policy (RSE-PTO-01), the Holiday Calendar & Observance policy (RSE-HOL-01) and the Leave of Absence Policy (RSE-LV-01). For example, a PTO request of 11 consecutive working days needs the director, while a request of 10 or fewer needs only the manager.

## 3 Work Location and Travel

| Request | Approver |
|---|---|
| Temporary remote work, same state/province | None (tell your manager) |
| Temporary remote work, other US state/province, up to 30 calendar days/year | Manager |
| Temporary remote work, other US state/province, over 30 calendar days/year | HR |

| Change to remote-approved | Manager + HR |

The rules behind these rows are in the Remote & Hybrid Work Policy (RSE-RW-01) and the Work Location, Travel & Cross-Border Work Guidelines (RSE-LOC-01). Requests to work from another country go to HR's Cross-Border Review through an HR ticket; a manager's support is welcome but is not an approval.

## 6 Exceptions

Any exception to a Ridgeline policy is approved by the Director of People & HR, Renata Alvarez. Ask the HR Service Desk to start an exception request.

Explain what you are asking for, which rule it departs from and why. Exceptions are granted only for good reason and are recorded, so that similar cases are treated consistently. An exception for one person does not change the policy for anyone else.
