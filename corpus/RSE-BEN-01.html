1 Eligibility and Coverage

1.1 Who is eligible

Benefits eligibility depends on your Employment Type. Full-time employees are eligible for all US plans in §2. Part-time employees are eligible for medical, dental, vision and the 401(k); Ridgeline's employer contribution to medical premiums is pro-rated by scheduled hours. Interns are eligible for the 401(k) only. Contractors are not eligible for Ridgeline benefits. Employees of the Toronto office are covered by the RSE Canada Group Plan instead of the US plans (§5).

Your eligibility is based on the Employment Type on your HR record. If your Employment Type changes, for example from part-time to full-time, your eligibility changes from the date of the change, and you can update your elections within the qualifying life event window (§3.4).

1.2 When coverage starts

Coverage starts on the first day of the month after your hire date. If you enrol within your new-hire window (§3.1) after that date has passed, your coverage is back-dated to the first day of the month after your hire date.

For example, an employee hired in the middle of October is covered from the first day of November once they enrol, even if they enrol a few days into November. Until you enrol, you have no Ridgeline coverage, so enrol early in your window.

1.3 Coverage levels

For medical, dental and vision you choose one coverage level: employee only, employee plus spouse, employee plus children, or family.

You can choose a different coverage level for each plan, for example family medical coverage and employee-only vision. Your coverage level stays the same for the plan year unless you have a qualifying life event (§3.4).

3 Enrolling and Making Changes

3.1 New hires

New hires have 30 calendar days from their hire date to enrol in benefits through the HR Portal. For example, an employee hired on October 12 can enrol until November 11. Coverage starts as described in §1.2.

Enrol in the HR Portal under Benefits. Choose your plans and coverage levels, add any family members you want to cover, and confirm. If you don't want a plan, you can waive it. Your choices then apply for the rest of the plan year.

3.2 If you miss the window

If you miss your new-hire window, you can next enrol at open enrollment, unless you have a qualifying life event (§3.4) before then.

HR cannot extend the new-hire window, so missing it can leave you without Ridgeline coverage until the next plan year. If you are close to the end of your window and unsure what to choose, enrol in a plan now; you can change your choices at open enrollment.

3.3 Open enrollment

Open enrollment for 2027 runs from November 16 to November 30, 2026. Choices made at open enrollment take effect on January 1, 2027. Open enrollment is the time to join, change or leave a plan for the coming year.

HR sends a reminder before open enrollment starts, with any changes to the plans for the coming year. If you make no changes, your current elections generally continue into the new year. Check your family members and coverage levels each year.

3.4 Qualifying life events

Certain life events let you change your benefits outside open enrollment: the birth or adoption of a child, marriage, and loss of other coverage. Make the change in the HR Portal within 31 days of the event; for example, add a new baby within 31 days of the birth.

The change you make must be consistent with the event: a marriage lets you add your spouse, and the birth of a child lets you add the child and change your coverage level. HR may ask for documents that confirm the event. Changes take effect from the date of the event where the plan allows.

4 Benefits During Leave

During paid leave, such as paid parental leave, your benefits continue on the same terms and your contributions continue through payroll. If you take unpaid leave of more than 30 days, contact HR before the leave starts to arrange how your contributions will be paid.

Shorter unpaid leave doesn't change your benefits. When you return from any leave, your benefits continue as before; you don't need to re-enrol. Ask HR how a specific leave affects your 401(k) contributions, since contributions are based on pay received.

5 Canada: RSE Canada Group Plan

Employees of the Toronto office are covered by the RSE Canada Group Plan, which provides health, dental and other group benefits for Canadian employees. This summary does not describe the plan in detail. Plan details, claims and eligibility questions for the RSE Canada Group Plan go to HR.

The US plan descriptions in §2 do not apply to Toronto employees, even where the plans have similar names. Toronto employees enrol in the RSE Canada Group Plan through HR when they join, and HR provides the plan booklet and claim forms.
