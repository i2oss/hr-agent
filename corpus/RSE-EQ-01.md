## 1 Standard Equipment

Every employee gets a standard kit from IT: a laptop, two monitors and a headset. Field/site-based employees also get a rugged tablet for site work. IT has your equipment ready before your first day (RSE-ONB-01 §1). Interns receive the standard kit for the length of their internship.

The kit is set up by IT with the software and security settings you need. Office-hybrid employees keep their laptop with them and use shared monitors on their anchor days; ask IT if you need monitors at home too. Specialist equipment, such as a high-performance workstation for analysis or modelling, is arranged through your manager and IT.

## 2 Home-Office Stipend

### 2.1 Remote-approved

Remote-approved employees can claim a one-time home-office stipend of $500 toward a desk, chair, lighting or similar items for their home workspace.

The stipend is one-time: it is not repeated each year. An employee who already received the office-hybrid stipend and later becomes remote-approved can claim the difference between the two amounts. Items bought with the stipend are yours to keep.

### 2.2 Office-hybrid

Office-hybrid employees can claim a one-time home-office stipend of $250 for their remote days.

Suitable items include a desk chair, a monitor arm, a desk lamp or a keyboard. The stipend can't be used for furniture unrelated to work or for general household items.

### 2.3 Not eligible

Field/site-based employees, interns and contractors are not eligible for a home-office stipend.

### 2.4 Internet

Remote-approved employees can also claim $50 per month toward home internet. Office-hybrid employees cannot claim internet costs.

### 2.5 How to claim

Claim the stipend through an expense claim within 90 days of becoming eligible, with receipts. Your manager approves the claim (RSE-APR-01 §5).

You become eligible on the day your Work Arrangement starts, or on your hire date if you join with that arrangement. The internet allowance is claimed monthly as an expense, with the bill attached.

## 3 Personal Equipment

Personal laptops are not reimbursed, and they may not hold Ridgeline project files (RSE-SEC-01 §3.3). Use the Ridgeline laptop you were issued for all project work.

Personal accessories such as your own headphones or keyboard may be used with Ridgeline equipment, but they are not reimbursed outside the stipend in §2. If your Ridgeline laptop isn't suitable for your work, ask IT for a replacement rather than using a personal machine.

## 4 Care, Loss and Return

Look after Ridgeline equipment as if it were your own. Report a lost or stolen device as set out in RSE-SEC-01 §3.4. When you leave Ridgeline, or at the end of an internship, return all equipment to IT.

Report damage to IT promptly so it can be repaired or replaced. Accidental damage is not charged to you. Remote-approved employees can return equipment by courier, arranged by IT. Don't wipe or reset a Ridgeline device yourself; IT does this so that project data is handled correctly.
