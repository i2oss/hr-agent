## 1 Your Work Location

### 1.1 What "work location" means

Your work location is the work region and country on your HR record, for example Colorado, US or Ontario, Canada. Your work location decides which rules apply when you work from somewhere else, whether that is business travel (§2) or temporary remote work (RSE-RW-01 §6).

For most employees the work location is the state or province of their home office. Remote-approved employees may have a different work region, which is recorded when their arrangement is approved. Keep your work location up to date: it affects payroll, benefits and which holiday calendar applies.

## 2 Business Travel

### 2.1 Pre-approval

Your manager must pre-approve every business trip before you book it.

Ask for approval with the purpose of the trip, the dates, the destination and an estimate of the main costs. Approval covers the business part of the trip; personal days added to it are requested separately as PTO (§2.4).

### 2.2 Booking

Book flights, hotels and rental cars through Ridgeline Travel, the internal booking portal. Travel costs are reimbursed under the Travel & Expense Reimbursement policy (RSE-EXP-01).

### 2.3 Business travel vs temporary remote work

Travel to do Ridgeline work at a client, a project site or a Ridgeline office is business travel, not temporary remote work. A business trip of up to 14 calendar days needs no Cross-Border Review, even when it is to another country. Longer stays, or working from another country for your own reasons, follow §3.

For example, a week at the Toronto office for project meetings is business travel for a Denver employee: the manager pre-approves it and no Cross-Border Review is needed. Spending several weeks working from a family home abroad is not business travel, even if you do your normal job while there.

### 2.4 Adding personal days to a trip

You may add personal days to a business trip. Personal days are taken as PTO under the Paid Time Off Policy (RSE-PTO-01), and the costs of the personal days are yours (RSE-EXP-01 §6).

Request the PTO in the usual way, with the usual notice and approval. Your holiday calendar is still your home office's calendar while you travel. Adding a few personal days doesn't turn a business trip into temporary remote work, provided you don't work on those days.

## 3 Working from Another Country

### 3.1 Always reviewed by HR

Working from another country, other than on a short business trip (§2.3), is always reviewed by HR, whatever the length. Your manager cannot approve it alone.

### 3.2 Annual cap

You may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Your manager cannot approve anything over the cap; HR reviews it as an exception, and it may not be approved.

Working days are counted on your home office calendar, excluding weekends and your company holidays. The cap counts all working days from another country in the calendar year, across every request. A stay of six weeks, for example, is around 30 working days and is over the cap.

### 3.3 Request at least 28 calendar days ahead

Request work from another country at least 28 calendar days before you plan to start, so HR has time to complete the review.

Requests made with less notice may still be reviewed, but HR may not be able to decide in time, and the request is more likely to be declined.

### 3.4 Security requirements

Working from another country has extra security requirements, including notifying IT Security in advance; see RSE-SEC-01 §5.4.

HR approval and IT Security's checks are separate steps, and both are needed before you go. Use the VPN at all times while abroad.

## 4 Cross-Border Review

### 4.1 What HR reviews

The Cross-Border Review is how HR checks a request to work from another country. HR looks at tax, immigration and work authorization, insurance, and payroll. Ridgeline does not give personal tax, immigration or legal advice; the review decides whether Ridgeline can support the request, and you may want your own adviser for personal questions.

The review protects both you and Ridgeline: working in another country can create obligations that neither side intended. HR may approve a request, approve it with conditions such as shorter dates, or decline it. HR does not tell you what you personally owe in tax or which visa you need.

### 4.2 How to request

Open an HR ticket in the category remote_work, with your dates, the country and your work plan.

Your work plan should say what work you will do, how you will meet core hours across time zones and how you will keep project data secure. Tell your manager you have opened the ticket. HR's Cross-Border Review queue picks it up and may ask you for more detail.

### 4.3 Timeline

HR gives a decision within 10 working days of receiving a complete request.

The clock starts when HR has everything it needs, so include your dates, country and work plan in the ticket. Don't book non-refundable travel until you have the decision.

## 5 Moving Permanently

Contact HR before any permanent change of work location, to another state, province or country. A permanent move changes your HR record and may not be possible for every role.

A move can change your payroll, benefits, holiday calendar and Work Arrangement. HR will explain what changes and what needs to be approved. Moving before HR has agreed can leave Ridgeline unable to employ you in the new location.
