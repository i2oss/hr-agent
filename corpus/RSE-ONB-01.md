## 1 Before Day One

Before your first day, send HR the documents listed in your offer pack so your HR record and payroll can be set up. Your manager arranges your desk or site induction, and IT prepares your standard equipment (RSE-EQ-01 §1) so it is ready when you arrive.

Your manager also assigns you an onboarding buddy, a colleague from your team who can answer everyday questions in your first months. Field/site-based employees complete a site safety induction before their first site visit.

## 2 Your First Week

### 2.1 Security training

Complete Ridgeline's information security training within 5 working days of your start date. Your access to Confidential and Client Restricted data depends on it.

The training covers data classification, device rules, MFA and how to report an incident, as set out in RSE-SEC-01. It is online and takes about an hour. Your manager is told when you have completed it.

### 2.2 HR Portal setup

Log in to the HR Portal in your first week and check your profile, set up time off and review your benefits options.

Check that your Employment Type, Work Arrangement, office and manager are recorded correctly, since they decide which policies apply to you. Add your emergency contacts and read and acknowledge the Employee Handbook (RSE-HB-01 §8).

### 2.3 Benefits enrollment

You have a 30-day window from your hire date to enrol in benefits; see RSE-BEN-01 §3.1. Don't leave it to the last week.

## 3 Your First 90 Days

### 3.1 Check-ins

Your manager holds check-ins with you at 30, 60 and 90 days to review how you're settling in, your goals and any support you need.

Use the check-ins to raise anything that isn't working, from equipment to workload. HR may join the final check-in. After your first 90 days, you move to Ridgeline's normal cycle of regular one-to-ones with your manager.

### 3.2 Remote-approved arrangements

New employees can't become remote-approved before 6 months of service; see RSE-RW-01 §3. Office-hybrid employees follow the anchor-day pattern from the start.

Your first months in the office help you learn how Ridgeline works, meet colleagues across disciplines and build the relationships that make remote work successful later. Talk to your manager about your longer-term working pattern at your 90-day check-in.

### 3.3 Using PTO

You can use PTO as soon as it has accrued, including during your first 90 days. Request it in the HR Portal as usual.

Because PTO accrues each pay period, your balance in your first weeks is small. If you already have time off booked when you join, for example a holiday arranged before your offer, tell your manager and HR before your start date so it can be agreed; HR can discuss unpaid personal leave if your balance won't cover it.

## 4 Who to Contact

For HR questions, contact the HR Service Desk at hr@ridgeline.example or through HR Portal > Ask HR. For equipment and access problems, contact IT. For day-to-day questions about your work, start with your manager.

For sensitive concerns, use HR Confidential (RSE-HB-01 §6.1). For security incidents, contact IT Security straight away (RSE-SEC-01 §7.1). Your onboarding buddy can help you find your way around tools, teams and offices.
